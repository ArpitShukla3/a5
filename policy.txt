A. Appointments and Cancellations
Appointments may be booked online, by telephone or in person. A deposit of GBP 50 is taken for all aesthetics appointments and GBP 25 for dental hygiene appointments. Patients must give at least 24 hours notice to cancel or move an appointment. An appointment may be moved once free of charge where more than 24 hours notice is given. Patients who do not attend and give no notice are charged the full cost of the booked treatment.

B. Fees and Payments
Payment is taken at the time of treatment unless the patient is covered by an approved insurer. Meridian accepts debit and credit cards and bank transfer. Cash payments above GBP 500 are not accepted. Balances unpaid after 30 days are referred to a third-party collections agency and a further GBP 25 is added. Itemised invoices are issued on request within five working days.

C. Refunds and Deposits
A refund may be requested within 14 days of payment where treatment has been paid for but not delivered. Deposits are non-refundable in all circumstances. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within ten working days. Where a patient is unhappy with the clinical outcome of a treatment, this is handled under the complaints procedure and not as a refund request.

D. Patient Records and Data Protection
Meridian processes patient data under UK GDPR. Patients may request a copy of their record at any time. Requests are answered within 30 days and there is no charge. Adult patient records are retained for eight years after the date of the last appointment. Information about Meridian staff, including the reasons for any absence, is confidential and is not disclosed to patients under any circumstances.

E. Aesthetic Treatments
Written consent is required before any aesthetic treatment and is signed at the consultation. For injectable treatments there is a cooling-off period of 14 days between the consultation and the first treatment, and treatment cannot be carried out on the same day as a first consultation. Where a patient decides not to proceed during the cooling-off period, the deposit is refunded in full on request. Meridian does not provide aesthetic or injectable treatments to anyone under the age of 18, including with parental consent.

F. Staff Absence and Rescheduling
Where a clinician is unavailable, affected patients are contacted at the earliest opportunity and offered an alternative appointment. Where a clinician's absence continues beyond two weeks, affected patients are contacted individually and offered either transfer to another clinician or a full refund of any deposit held. An absence of this length is recorded as extended leave. Extended leave covers medical leave, compassionate leave and parental leave. No charge applies to any appointment cancelled by Meridian.

H. Complaints
Complaints should be made in writing to the Practice Manager at the site concerned. A complaint is acknowledged within three working days. A full written response is provided within 20 working days. A complaint must be raised within six months of the event complained about. Where a patient remains dissatisfied after the full response, the complaint may be escalated to the Independent Sector Complaints Adjudication Service.
